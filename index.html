Practice and resources

Exercises to work through for Database Systems — modeling, the relational model, SQL, normalization, relational algebra and Datalog. One page per topic, added as the semester goes on.

Every exercise carries a full worked solution, hidden until you click. Printing a page opens all of them.

Exercise sets

Each set follows a topic we have covered in class. Work the problems on paper first — the solution is one click away, and reading it before you have tried is the fastest way to feel confident about something you cannot actually do.

  1. 1ER modeling: drawing the diagramPlain ER — entity sets, relationships, attributes, cardinality and participation. What deserves to be an entity set and what is only a value; weak entity sets and partial keys; and where an attribute belongs when it describes a pair rather than a thing. Three short warm-ups and sixteen complete diagrams to draw, ending with two large ones — the whole course schedule, and the bibliography database. 19 exercises.
  2. 2EER: subclasses and specializationThe enhanced model — subclasses, superclasses and inheritance; the disjointness and completeness constraints, and how to read each one off a sentence; where an attribute or a relationship belongs once a hierarchy exists. One warm-up and eight complete diagrams, ending with the Pure Wood database. 9 exercises.
More coming

Sets for the relational model, SQL, normalization theory, relational algebra, Datalog and storage structures will appear here as we reach them.

How to use this page

Try, then check

Every exercise is a click-to-open box. The solution shows the whole method, not just the answer, so you can find the exact step where your work diverged.

Print it

Printing a page — or saving it as a PDF — opens every solution and drops the navigation. That gives you a clean problem set with a full answer key.

Dark mode

The ◖ button in the top right switches between light and dark, and remembers your choice.
